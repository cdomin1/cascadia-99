THE BASICS

Swap. Chain. Survive.

  1. Swap adjacent panels. Move the two-panel cursor with the arrow keys; press Space to swap. You can also tap the board and use the touch buttons.
  2. Match three or more. Line up matching glyphs horizontally or vertically. Four shapes identify the tiles even without color. Panels fall into empty spaces after a clear.
  3. Build a chain. If falling panels make another match, your chain grows. Matches of 4+ and chains send garbage to a rival.
  4. Defend your board. Attacks arrive after four seconds as full-width carbon slabs. Your attacks cancel incoming garbage first. Clear next to a slab to release new tiles.
  5. Stay below the ceiling. The stack rises over time. Hold Shift to speed it up. Stay at the top for two seconds and you’re out.
  6. Charge a Pulse. Clearing panels fills your energy. At 100%, press X or Pulse to cancel up to six incoming blocks. In teams, if your queue is empty, Pulse protects your teammate.
  7. Outlast the room. Pick a targeting mode or click a rival. In 2P, 4P, and Battle Royale, the last player alive wins. In 2v2, the last team with a survivor wins—even an eliminated teammate shares the win.

Choose 2P duels, 4P free-for-all, 2v2 teams, or a Battle Royale with up to 99 players. Fill seats with CPUs on Easy, Normal, or Hard. Rush rules make the stack rise 60% faster. All players need the same game server address and room code.
