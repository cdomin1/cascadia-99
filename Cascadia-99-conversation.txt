### Visual Design Spec: Minimalist 4-Tile System & Aesthetics

#### 1. Core Visual Goals
- Modern, clean, and minimalist aesthetic avoiding Nintendo (*Panel de Pon*) and Tetris trade dress.
- Immediate peripheral readability for fast-paced 99-player multiplayer.
- High accessibility: distinct internal glyph silhouettes to ensure clarity under grayscale and colorblind conditions.

---

#### 2. Tile Geometry & Container Spec
- **Tile Base:** Rounded rectangle (squircle).
- **Corner Radius:** 16% of tile width (prevents diamond gaps at tile intersections while maintaining a modern, soft feel).
- **Glyph Rendering:** Centered inside the container; stamped in high-contrast solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or 90% opacity off-white (`#F8F9FA`).
- **Stroke / Visual Weight:** Uniform stroke thickness across all 4 internal glyphs so no single tile draws disproportionate visual weight.

---

#### 3. Tile Set & Color Palette (Neo-Arcade High Contrast)

| Piece ID | Internal Glyph | Glyph Description | Color Name | Hex Code | Container Tint Hex |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tile 1** | Crescent | Upward-curving solid crescent / arc | **Coral Tangerine** | `#FF5E5B` | `#D94542` |
| **Tile 2** | Diagonal Slash | 45-degree angled bar with rounded caps (`/`) | **Electric Cobalt** | `#2070FF` | `#1753C4` |
| **Tile 3** | Trefoil | 3-lobed radial pinwheel (120-degree symmetry) | **Mint Sage** | `#00E5A3` | `#00B380` |
| **Tile 4** | Stacked Dots | 3 vertically aligned solid dots (`⋮`) | **Solar Ochre** | `#FFAE03` | `#CC8B02` |

---

#### 4. Garbage / Incoming Attack Block Spec
- **Geometry:** Rectangular solid slabs matching board width, with hard 90-degree corners (sharp contrast to player tiles).
- **Surface Styling:** Dark semi-matte slate/carbon fiber (`#1E222B`) with subtle horizontal hazard accent lines or translucent frosted-glass styling.
- **Explicit Rule:** Do NOT use classic grey metallic stone with embossed face sprites or round rivets.

---

#### 5. Selection Cursor & Visual States
- **Cursor:** Dual-tile horizontal bounding capsule (2x1 grid size) with rounded corners matching the tile radius. Use a clean outer glow or 2px bright outline (`#FFFFFF` with subtle drop shadow), snapping smoothly between tile pairs.
- **Match / Clear Animation:** Brief high-luminance flash where rounded corners snap square for 3 frames before bursting into outward particles.
- **Top-Out / Danger State:** Subtle pulse on column tiles crossing the top 80% threshold without full-screen red tint clutter. let's update the shapes and color palette based on this, also update the animated gif on home page

### Visual Design Spec: Incoming Garbage / Attack Slabs

#### 1. Geometry & Framing
- **Outer Shell:** Hard, unyielding rectangles (strict 90° corners or an ultra-tight 4% chamfer). This creates immediate visual contrast against the soft 16% rounded corners of active player tiles.
- **Sizing:** Spans full-width (6 columns) or multi-column segments (3 to 5 columns), with variable height (1 to 3 rows) based on incoming attack magnitude.
- **Surface Material:** Deep dark slate / matte carbon weave (`#1A1D24` base with `#262B36` subtle inner bevel).

#### 2. Surface Details & Graphic Elements
- **Hazard Linework:** Thin 45° diagonal hazard hash marks or subtle dual-stripe accent bars along the top edge (`#FF3B30` or high-vis Amber `#FF9500`) to signal threat level.
- **Segment Dividers:** Recessed vertical seam lines every 1-column interval so the player can still read column alignment beneath the slab.
- **Lock Core / Glyph Anchor:** A centered, low-contrast geometric lock bracket or breaker node (`#3A404D`) instead of cartoon eyes or faces.

#### 3. State & Clear Transitions
- **Dormant / Falling State:** Dark, heavy, opaque, with a subtle low-opacity drop shadow casting downward onto the stack beneath it.
- **Activated / Shatter-Prep State (When an adjacent match hits):**
  - The dark slate instantly turns semi-translucent frosted glass, revealing glowing fracture seams.
  - A bright white line sweeps across the segment seams.
- **Breakdown State:** Fractures into standard active tiles from bottom-to-top, adopting the squircle shape and assigned palette colors as the layer peels away.
